A game about US manufacturing

Build a solar factory.
Survive to 2030.

Pick a state, a stage, your suppliers and a size. Then the real shocks arrive one quarter at a time: duty orders, tax-credit rules, grid connection waits, ramp-up problems, price crashes. Every number on the shocks comes from a public source. Many players do not make it.

The stack of wafers is your cash. About six minutes. Works on a phone. Nothing is saved anywhere except your result card.
